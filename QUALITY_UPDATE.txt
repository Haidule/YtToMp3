🚀 HOW TO USE:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Run the app:
   $ ./run.sh

2. Paste YouTube URL

3. Select quality:
   - 128 kbps for smaller files
   - 192 kbps for balanced quality (recommended)
   - 320 kbps for best audio quality

4. Click "Download and Convert to MP3"

💡 RECOMMENDATIONS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Music from YouTube:
→ Use 192 kbps or 320 kbps
  (YouTube audio is typically 128-256 kbps, so 320 captures max quality)

Podcasts/Spoken Word:
→ Use 128 kbps
  (Speech doesn't need high bitrate, saves space)

General Downloads:
→ Use 192 kbps (default)
  (Good balance of quality and file size)
